## 🤔 Common Questions

### "Should I focus on features or fixing the 12 failing tests first?"
**Ship features.** Tests are important, but users don't care about tests - they care about value. Fix tests in parallel, but prioritize user-facing features.

### "Should I build a marketplace now or later?"
**Later.** First prove that people love using the tool, THEN give them a way to share. Marketplace without users is a ghost town.

### "Should I charge money now?"
**Not yet.** First build something people love for free. Once you have 1,000+ happy users, introduce paid tiers. You can't charge for something people don't love yet.

### "Which Quick Win should I start with?"
**Quality Scoring (QW2).** It's the easiest to implement, has immediate impact, and builds foundation for later features. Don't overthink it - just ship it.

### "How do I know if users are 'wowed'?"
**You'll know.** Look for:
- Unsolicited tweets saying "This is amazing!"
- GitHub stars growing 10%+ per week
- People asking "When can I pay for this?"
- Contributors submitting PRs without being asked
- Blog posts/videos from community
